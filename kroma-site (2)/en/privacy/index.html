Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

Kroma has no accounts, shows no ads, uses no usage analytics and sends none of your data to any server. Everything you create and import stays on your device.

Who we are

Kroma (the “app”) lets keyboard players perform live with SoundFont and Decent Sampler sounds. It is published by [YOUR NAME OR COMPANY]. Contact: [your-email@domain.com].

Data the app handles

We do not collect personal data. The app stores, only in your device’s storage:

  • Live Sets, setlists, scenes, presets and settings;
  • sounds and audio files you import (SoundFont, Decent Sampler, SFZ, WAV, etc.);
  • recordings made inside the app.

None of it leaves your device unless you export, back up or share a file. Uninstalling the app removes the internal data through Android.

What the app does not do

  • No sign-up, login or email required.
  • No advertising and no third-party analytics or crash-reporting tools.
  • No access to contacts, location, camera, microphone or your device’s file listing.
  • No selling or sharing of information with third parties.

Wi-Fi remote control

The optional “Remote” feature links two devices running Kroma on the same local network, with no internet and no server of ours. Packets carry only touch commands and the names of the FX and Pad squares, protected by a 4-digit code shown on the main device. That is why the app declares the network permission. With the feature off, the app does not use the network.

Android permissions

  • Network: only for the local-network Remote.
  • Foreground service, wake lock and notifications: keep audio playing with the screen off and show the session notification.
  • Do Not Disturb (optional): Show Mode can enable it; you grant this in system settings.
  • Vibration: haptic feedback on taps and faders.
  • Bluetooth: connect Bluetooth MIDI keyboards and controllers, if you use that feature.
  • Files: the app only reads files you pick with the system file picker.

Sharing and external links

When you export a recording or a Live Set, the file is handed to the app you choose in Android’s share menu and is then governed by that app’s policy. The support and policy links open your email app or browser.

Children

The app is not directed to children and does not collect data from anyone, including minors.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing in our systems to access, correct or delete. Questions about your rights (GDPR, or Brazil’s LGPD) can be sent to the contact below.

Changes and contact
